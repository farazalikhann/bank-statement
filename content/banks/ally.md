## What an Ally Bank statement PDF looks like

Ally is an online-only bank with no physical branches, and its statements reflect that: Ally Interest Checking and Ally Online Savings statements are generated by a modern digital platform without decades of legacy branch-banking statement formats to carry forward or reconcile between acquisitions. In practice, that means a single continuous chronological transaction table — Date, Description, Amount, running Balance — without the multi-section deposits/withdrawals splits common among older, branch-heavy national banks, and without the format inconsistencies that can show up in institutions built from multiple mergers.

Ally Online Savings statements are simpler still, since savings accounts typically see far fewer transactions per period (Ally, like most banks, limits certain types of savings withdrawals per statement cycle), often just a handful of transfers and interest payments per month.

## Where to download it

In Ally's online banking or mobile app, go to the account and select **Statements & Documents** (sometimes under **Account Details**). PDF statements are available going back to account opening for most Ally accounts, since Ally has been online-only from the start and doesn't have a pre-digital paper-statement history to worry about.

## Date format and columns

Ally prints a full `MM/DD/YYYY` date per transaction with description, signed amount, and running balance — a straightforward single-table format that generally converts cleanly without needing to combine multiple sections.

## Common issues when converting

Because the layout is consistently simple, the main things worth double-checking are less about Ally's format itself and more about interest-payment rows, which some users don't think of as "real transactions" but which do appear in the transaction list and do affect the running balance — excluding them from a converted spreadsheet by hand (rather than exporting the full statement as-is) will break the balance reconciliation, since the printed closing balance already accounts for that period's interest. If you're specifically trying to isolate spending activity and exclude interest, do that as a separate filtering step after confirming the full statement reconciles, not before.
